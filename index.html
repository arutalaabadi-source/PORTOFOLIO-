01 / QCC WAREHOUSE • OTD

Improving On-Time Delivery Through Warehouse Redesign

Redesigned the storage system from floor storage to a racking system to improve material accessibility, storage organization, space utilization and retrieval efficiency, supporting better On-Time Delivery performance.

PROBLEM

Floor storage created inefficient space usage and made material identification, access and handling less effective.

IMPROVEMENT

Developed a racking-based storage concept with clearer locations and improved material accessibility.

METHOD

Quality Control Circle, warehouse observation and process improvement analysis.

IMPACT

Better warehouse organization, easier retrieval and stronger support for On-Time Delivery.
