WAREHOUSE • OTD

Improving On-Time Delivery Through Warehouse Redesign

Redesigned the warehouse storage system from floor storage to a racking-based concept to improve material accessibility, storage organization, space utilization, and retrieval efficiency.

PROBLEM

Floor storage resulted in inefficient space utilization and made material identification, access, and handling less effective.

IMPROVEMENT

Developed a racking-based storage concept with clearer locations and improved material accessibility.

METHOD

Quality Control Circle (QCC), warehouse observation, layout analysis, and process improvement.

IMPACT

Improved warehouse organization, easier material retrieval, and stronger support for On-Time Delivery.
